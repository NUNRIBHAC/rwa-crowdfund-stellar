# Testnet FAQ

## Do I need real money?

No. The platform runs on the Stellar testnet. All balances and
contributions use test assets with no monetary value.

## How do I get testnet funds?

Varial (formerly Freighter) can fund a testnet account from Friendbot,
the Stellar testnet faucet. Test campaigns use testnet USDC, which the demo environment
provides through its own test flow.

## What is the difference between Connect and Sign in?

**Connect** shares your public wallet address so the app can prepare
investment transactions - nothing is signed. **Sign in** opens your account
area: you sign a one-time challenge message to prove you control the
wallet. See [Getting started](./getting-started).

## Are contributions refundable?

Funding is all-or-nothing. If a campaign does not reach its goal by its
deadline, contributions can be reclaimed. If the goal is met, funds pay out
to the issuer after the deadline.

## Is my wallet safe?

Your keys never leave your wallet (Varial/Freighter). The platform cannot move funds; every
transaction is signed by you in the wallet.

## Where can I learn about the risks?

Read the risk disclosure on the
[demo site](https://blockallisto-demo.onrender.com). Nothing on this portal
or the demo is investment advice.
