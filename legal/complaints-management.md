# Complaints Management Policy

**Last updated:** [date to be set on publication]

This page explains how to complain about the Blockallisto prototype and how complaints are handled. Blockallisto today is a demo on the Stellar testnet with no real funds involved; the handling process below is real for the demo, while the formal regulatory framework it mirrors applies only to an authorised platform.

## 1. How to complain

Email blockallisto@gmail.com with the subject "Complaint". Include: what happened, when, the page or feature involved, and what outcome you are asking for. Complaints are free of charge.

For the prototype there is also a support ticket form on the site; complaints sent there are handled the same way.

## 2. What happens next

- **Acknowledgement:** we confirm receipt within [10 business days - template value].
- **Investigation:** the operator reviews the complaint against records (page content, contract events, support history). You may be asked for clarification.
- **Answer:** a reasoned reply within [20 business days - template value] of acknowledgement. If more time is needed, we say so and explain why.
- **Record:** complaints and outcomes are logged in a complaints register kept for [5 years - template value], containing the complaint, the investigation and the resolution.

Template timelines are indicative values drawn from common EU platform practice, not legal commitments; final values require legal advice.

## 3. If you are not satisfied

For the prototype there is no supervisory escalation path, because the operator is not an authorised crowdfunding service provider and no real investment service exists. For a future authorised platform, this section will name the competent authority's complaint channel (in Portugal, the CMVM) and any applicable out-of-court dispute-resolution body before any real offer is made.

## 4. Using complaints to improve

Recurring complaint themes are reviewed and feed into product and content fixes. The prototype's own roadmap treats complaints data as an input to design changes, not just case-by-case resolution.
