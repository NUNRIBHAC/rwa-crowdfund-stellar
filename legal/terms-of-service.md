## 1. What Blockallisto is today

Blockallisto is a **prototype demonstration** of a tokenized real-world-asset crowdfunding concept, running entirely on the **Stellar test network (testnet)**.

This means, plainly:

- The Service is **not** a crowdfunding platform in the legal sense. We are **not** an authorised crowdfunding service provider under Regulation (EU) 2020/1503 (ECSPR) or any other regime, and we make **no live offers** of any investment.
- Nothing on the Service is an offer, a solicitation, a recommendation, or investment, legal, tax or financial advice, in any jurisdiction.
- No real money is involved. Contributions on the demo use Stellar **testnet tokens (test USDC) that have no monetary value** and cannot be exchanged for real funds.
- Sample campaigns are fictional illustrations. Issuer identities, valuations, figures and outcomes shown on the demo are **mock data**, not real opportunities.

## 2. What the Service lets you do

- Browse and filter sample investment campaigns and read their structured terms.
- Use the learning hub, glossary and FAQ to understand investment instruments.
- Connect a **Freighter** wallet (a browser extension) and make **test contributions** to the demo campaign on the Stellar testnet.

Test contributions are handled by a Soroban smart contract with the following behaviour: all-or-nothing funding, payout to the campaign only after its deadline, contributions capped at the campaign target, and balances recorded in the contract. No transferable token is issued.

## 3. Planned features (not yet live)

Blockallisto is being designed against the compliance surfaces of established EU crowdfunding platforms. Future versions **may** include: user accounts and registration; identity verification (KYC/AML), including beneficial-owner and source-of-funds checks for company investors; investor categorisation with an entry knowledge test and a loss-bearing-capacity simulation; a pre-contractual reflection period for non-sophisticated investors; key investment information sheets per campaign; payments processed by licensed payment service providers (never by us holding client funds); complaints management and conflicts-of-interest policies; and jurisdiction-based selling restrictions.

None of this exists today. The list above is indicative design direction drawn from our research corpus, not a specification: details, thresholds and whole features may change with design work and legal advice. These features will only launch under updated, legally reviewed terms, and only under the regulatory authorisations they require.
