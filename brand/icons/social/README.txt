Blockallisto - social network icons
31 Oct 2026 (revised)

Nine social networks: Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X, Threads,
Telegram, YouTube, Discord and Farcaster.

Folders
  svg/  vector icons (viewBox 24x24, brand colors)
  png/  ready raster icons, 512x512, transparent background

Where these files come from
  Icon geometry is from the Simple Icons project (simpleicons.org,
  CC0 1.0). LinkedIn is the exception: Simple Icons removed it at
  LinkedIn's request, so the included glyph is from that project's
  last archived version (v13.20.0). Farcaster's color is corrected
  to the official Farcaster Purple #8A63D2.

IMPORTANT - rights and per-platform rules
  These files are reference geometry, not permission. Each logo is a
  trademark of its owner and each platform sets its own rules:
  - Facebook (meta.com/brand/resources/facebook/logo/): blue logo on
    white, or white on Facebook Blue; never recolor; use only Meta's
    official download.
  - Instagram (meta.com/brand/resources/instagram/instagram-brand/):
    use only Meta's official assets; permission only for broadcast or
    large print.
  - LinkedIn (brand.linkedin.com/downloads): the most restrictive -
    no modifications, and LinkedIn says not to use logos obtained
    from third-party websites. Treat the included files as layout
    reference only and replace them with LinkedIn's official
    download before publishing. A social-icon row linking to your
    LinkedIn page is a permitted use.
  - X (about.x.com/en/who-we-are/brand-toolkit): logo in black or
    white only.
  - Threads (meta.com/brand/resources/instagram/threads/): official
    assets from Meta; clear space = icon width / 4.
  - Telegram (telegram.org/trademark): see Telegram's trademark terms.
  - YouTube (youtube.com/howyoutubeworks/resources/brand-resources/):
    icon in YouTube Red, Almost Black or White only; it may only link
    to a YouTube channel.
  - Discord (discord.com/branding): logo allowed in color, black or
    white; never edit, distort or reconfigure.
  - Farcaster: the logo was released under CC0 by Merkle Manufactory
    (see github.com/vrypan/farcaster-brand); purple, black or white.
