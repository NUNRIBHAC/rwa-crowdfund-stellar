When to use
Use this skill when a user needs to verify if a crowdfunding activity, platform structure, or investor disclosure meets the requirements of Regulation (EU) 2020/1503 (ECSPR). This includes drafting Key Investment Information Sheets (KIIS), determining authorization requirements, or applying investor protection rules.

## Instructions
1.  **Identify the Offering Type:** Determine if the project involves lending-based crowdfunding or investment-based crowdfunding (transferable securities).
2.  **Verify Thresholds:** Confirm the offer does not exceed the €5,000,000 total consideration threshold over a 12-month period.
3.  **Evaluate Investor Status:** Distinguish between 'sophisticated' and 'non-sophisticated' investors to apply appropriate protection levels.
4.  **Structure the KIIS:** Draft or review the Key Investment Information Sheet ensuring it contains the mandatory sections:
    *   Project owner details and main activities.
    *   Description of the crowdfunding process and conditions.
    *   Risk factors (specific to the project/issuer).
    *   Information on investor rights (e.g., pre-contractual reflection period).
5.  **Apply Disclosure Rules:** Ensure the "Entry Knowledge Test" and "Simulation of Ability to Bear Loss" are integrated for non-sophisticated investors.
6.  **Review Conflicts of Interest:** Check for forbidden activities, such as platforms accepting project owners they have a financial stake in.

## Examples
**User:** "We want to raise €2M for a solar farm. What do we need to tell non-sophisticated investors?"
**Agent:** "Under ECSPR, you must provide a Key Investment Information Sheet (KIIS). For non-sophisticated investors, you must also provide a 4-day pre-contractual reflection period where they can revoke their offer without penalty. You are also required to perform an 'Entry Knowledge Test' to ensure they understand the risks of solar infrastructure investments."

## Constraints
*   Do not provide legal advice; always include a disclaimer that the user should consult with qualified legal counsel or the relevant National Competent Authority (NCA).
*   Do not approve an offering that exceeds the €5M annual limit under ECSPR.
*   Do not suggest ways to bypass the mandatory 4-day reflection period for non-sophisticated investors.
*   Do not provide guidance for Reward-based or Donation-based crowdfunding, as these fall outside ECSPR scope.

## Purpose
Provide EU crowdfunding compliance guidance for a European Union tokenization platform using Stellar, Soroban smart contracts, stablecoins, or tokenized investment instruments.

## When This Skill Is Needed
Use this skill when the platform:
- connects investors with project owners seeking business funding
- offers investment-based or lending-based crowdfunding in the EU
- tokenizes crowdfunding instruments or investor records
- supports cross-border EU crowdfunding campaigns
- partners with an authorized European Crowdfunding Service Provider
- provides issuer onboarding, investor onboarding, subscriptions, payments, or campaign disclosures

## Core Responsibilities
- Assess whether the platform falls within ECSPR, Regulation (EU) 2020/1503.
- Determine whether the business needs ECSP authorization or should partner with an authorized provider.
- Review issuer/project owner onboarding requirements.
- Review investor protection flows, risk warnings, entry knowledge tests, and loss simulation.
- Review Key Investment Information Sheet, known as KIIS, processes.
- Review marketing communications for ECSPR compliance.
- Coordinate with MiCA / CASP Counsel, Securities Counsel, AML/CTF Officer, Data Privacy Counsel, and Payment Rails Specialist.
- Assess whether tokenized instruments may also trigger MiFID II, Prospectus Regulation, MiCA, or DLT Pilot Regime analysis.
- Support regulator-facing documentation and internal compliance procedures.
