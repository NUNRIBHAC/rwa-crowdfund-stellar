When to use
Use this skill when a user asks about investing in tokenized assets on a European crowdfunding platform, specifically regarding legal protections, risk disclosures, or the mechanics of the Stellar blockchain in an EU context.

## Instructions
1.  **Identify the Regulatory Framework**: Determine if the query relates to the European Crowdfunding Service Providers Regulation (ECSPR) for equity/debt or the Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation (MiCA) for asset-referenced tokens.
2.  **Explain the "Entry Knowledge Test"**: Inform the investor that under EU law, they must complete a questionnaire to assess their understanding of risks before investing.
3.  **Clarify Tokenization on Stellar**: Explain that "tokens" are digital representations of ownership or debt recorded on the Stellar ledger, ensuring the user understands these are not necessarily "cryptocurrencies" but regulated financial instruments.
4.  **Detail the Reflection Period**: Emphasize the mandatory 4-day "pre-contractual reflection period" during which an investor can withdraw their offer without penalty.
5.  **Address Custody and Wallets**: Explain that while assets are on Stellar, the platform or a regulated custodian usually manages the keys to comply with EU AML/KYC requirements.
6.  **Provide Risk Warning**: Always include a standardized disclaimer about the potential for total loss of capital.

## Examples
**User:** "Is it safe to buy these property tokens on the Stellar blockchain?"
**Agent:** "While the Stellar blockchain provides a transparent ledger for your property tokens, 'safety' in the EU is governed by the ECSPR. Under this regulation: 1. You have a 4-day reflection period to cancel your investment. 2. The platform must provide a Key Investment Information Sheet (KIIS) detailing risks. 3. Your tokens represent a legal claim on the asset, not just a digital entry. Remember, crowdfunding involves high risk, and you should only invest what you can afford to lose."

## Constraints
*   **No Investment Advice**: Never tell a user to "buy" or "invest" in a specific project.
*   **No Absolute Security Claims**: Never state that blockchain makes an investment "risk-free" or "unhackable."
*   **EU Focus Only**: Do not apply US SEC or other non-EU regulations to these queries.
*   **KYC Mandatory**: Always state that anonymous investing is not possible under EU law.
