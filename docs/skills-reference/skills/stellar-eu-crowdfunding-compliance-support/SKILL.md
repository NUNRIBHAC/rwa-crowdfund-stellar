When to use
Use this skill when a user asks about the operational requirements, regulatory constraints (ECSPR), or technical support procedures for a tokenized crowdfunding platform based in the European Union and operating on the Stellar blockchain.

## Instructions
1.  **Verify Regulation Status**: Always reference the European Crowdfunding Service Providers Regulation (ECSPR). Ensure the user understands that platforms must be authorized by their National Competent Authority (NCA).
2.  **Guide KYC/KYB**: Advise on the mandatory collection of identity documentation for investors and project owners as per EU Anti-Money Laundering (AML) directives.
3.  **Explain Stellar Assets**: Describe the issuance of "Asset Tokens" on Stellar (SEP-20/SEP-40) as representations of financial instruments.
4.  **Address Investor Categories**: Differentiate between "sophisticated" and "non-sophisticated" investors. For non-sophisticated investors, remind them of the mandatory "reflection period" (4 days) and the "Knowledge Test."
5.  **Technical Support for Wallets**: Provide guidance on setting up Stellar-compatible wallets (e.g., Albedo, Rabe, or Lobstr) and the necessity of establishing a "Trustline" for the platform's specific token.
6.  **Transparency Requirements**: Ensure the Key Investment Information Sheet (KIIS) is mentioned as a mandatory document for every offer.

## Examples
**User:** "How do I let a French investor buy tokens for my German solar project?"
**Agent:** "Under ECSPR, you can market across the EU once authorized. You must: 1. Ensure the investor passes a suitability test. 2. Provide them with the Key Investment Information Sheet (KIIS) in French or English. 3. Have them create a Stellar wallet and open a trustline for your specific Asset Code. 4. Collect their ID for AML/KYC before allowing them to deposit EUR via your payment bridge."

## Constraints
*   Do NOT provide specific legal or investment advice. Always include a disclaimer.
*   Do NOT recommend specific tokens or ICOs; focus on the platform operations.
*   Do NOT bypass EU AML/KYC requirements for "anonymity" reasons.
*   Do NOT suggest using the Stellar DEX for unregulated securities trading without a Multilateral Trading Facility (MTF) license.
