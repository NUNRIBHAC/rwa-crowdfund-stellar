When to use
Use this skill when an agent needs to provide tax guidance for projects launching crowdfunding campaigns in the European Union that utilize tokenization (Security Tokens or Utility Tokens) on the Stellar network. It is applicable for identifying VAT, Corporate Income Tax (CIT), and Capital Gains implications across EU member states.

## Instructions
1.  **Classify the Token Type**: Determine if the asset is a 'Security Token' (investment/dividend rights) or a 'Utility Token' (access to products).
2.  **Determine Jurisdiction**: Identify the specific EU member state where the issuer is tax-resident, as VAT and CIT rates vary.
3.  **Analyze VAT Liability**: 
    *   If a Utility Token: Assess if it is a single-purpose voucher (VAT due at issuance) or multi-purpose voucher (VAT due at redemption).
    *   If a Security Token: Generally exempt from VAT under EU finance exemptions.
4.  **Evaluate Corporate Income Tax (CIT)**: Advise on whether the funds raised are considered 'Revenue' (taxable immediately) or 'Equity/Debt' (not taxable as income).
5.  **Address Stellar Network Costs**: Account for the tax treatment of XLM transaction fees and base reserves as deductible business expenses.
6.  **Review MiCA Compliance**: Ensure tax reporting aligns with the Markets in Crypto-Assets (MiCA) regulation standards for transparency.

## Examples
**Scenario**: A French startup issues tokens on Stellar to fund a solar farm, promising a share of profits.
**Advisor Action**: 
1. Classify as a 'Security Token'.
2. Identify France as the jurisdiction (30% flat tax on capital gains for individuals).
3. Advise that the issuance is VAT-exempt.
4. Flag that profit distributions are subject to withholding tax.

## Constraints
*   Do not provide specific legal tax advice; always include a disclaimer that the user must consult a certified local tax professional.
*   Do not assume tax laws are identical across all EU member states; always check country-specific variations (e.g., Germany vs. Portugal).
*   Do not ignore DAC8 reporting requirements for crypto-asset service providers.
*   Do not provide advice for jurisdictions outside the European Union.
