# Tokenization infrastructure and software providers

Research checked on 2026-11-15. This is a provider reference list, not an endorsement, procurement decision, or legal opinion.

## Scope

These companies supply business-to-business software or infrastructure for issuing and administering tokenized assets. They are kept separate from the investment-platform inventories:

- [EU and US crowdfunding market research](market-research-eu-us-2026-10-29.md)
- [World market research outside the EU and US](market-research-world-excl-eu-us-2026-10-30.md)

The initial list contains **two providers: DigiShares and Brickken**. Neither is counted here as an investor-facing crowdfunding portal. A software feature, company registration, or sandbox participation does not establish authorization to provide regulated investment services.

## Verification status

- Both providers' own product and company/legal pages were read. The table reports their stated business and the limits of the evidence.
- No independent financial-regulator register confirmation was completed for either provider. This is not a finding that either company is unregulated, operating unlawfully, or exempt from all regulation.
- No Stellar integration was verified for either provider in this research. Do not assume Stellar support from general tokenization capabilities.
- No account was opened, software was trialled, contract was accepted, or integration was deployed. Prices, supported jurisdictions, production deployments, security controls, and contractual service levels were not validated.

## Checks before shortlisting a provider

1. Confirm the exact contracting entity, current product, supported networks, and asset/instrument types.
2. Separate the software provider from the issuer, crowdfunding service provider, broker, custodian, payment provider, and trading venue. Identify which entity performs each role and verify the relevant permissions in the applicable regulator's register.
3. Verify investor eligibility, transfer restrictions, ownership records, corporate actions, recovery procedures, data export, and the ability to migrate away from the provider.
4. Check security evidence, key custody, data protection, incident handling, pricing, and support terms against the intended deployment.

Additional providers should be added only with official sources and the same distinction between product claims and verified regulatory permissions.
